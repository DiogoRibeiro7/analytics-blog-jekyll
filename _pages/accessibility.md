We are committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of circumstance and ability.

- The theme uses semantic HTML landmarks, skip links, and accessible color contrast.
- All interactive controls provide visible focus indicators and descriptive labels.
- Content authors should include alternative text for images, captions for visualizations, and transcripts for audio/video.

If you encounter accessibility barriers, please contact us at [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).
