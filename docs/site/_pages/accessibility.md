We are committed to making the DataLog theme documentation accessible to everyone. The site follows WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines
by providing sufficient colour contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive link text, and skip links. If you spot an
issue or need an accommodation, email <a href="mailto:dfr@esmad.ipp.pt">dfr@esmad.ipp.pt</a> and we will respond within two
business days.
