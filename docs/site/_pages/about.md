The DataLog documentation project is maintained by the community around the theme. Contributors include analysts, data
scientists, and technical writers from academia and industry. We coordinate updates through the
[`analytics-blog-jekyll`](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/analytics-blog-jekyll) repository and publish releases to
[`datalog-theme.github.io`](https://datalog-theme.github.io).

Have a showcase to share or a migration scenario we should document? [Open an issue](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/analytics-blog-jekyll/issues)
and we will follow up.
