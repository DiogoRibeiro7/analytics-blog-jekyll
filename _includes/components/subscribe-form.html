Renders only when the site has a backend with the subscriptions feature on and `subscriptions.enabled` is not false; the footer and the post layout include it where `subscriptions.placement` says. `where` ("footer", "post" or "page", the default) keeps the ids apart when a page carries more than one, and picks the heading level.
